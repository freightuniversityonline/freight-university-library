About the Library

The Freight University Library exists for one reason: to keep the small American trucking company alive. Every volume here is written from the carrier-first doctrine — the belief that education, not luck, is what separates the businesses that survive from the ones that don't. New titles are added regularly. Everything is free; the only price of admission is telling us who you are, so we can keep teaching you.
